Shipping & Return Policy

Clear, honest policies to keep your experience smooth and satisfying.

Delivery Areas

We currently deliver across all major cities in India. Orders placed before 4 PM are usually dispatched the same day. Delivery time ranges from 2-5 business days depending on your location.

Return Policy

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery. Products must be unused, sealed, and in their original packaging. Return shipping charges are borne by the customer unless the item received is damaged or defective.

Refund Information

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive your returned item. The amount will be credited to your original payment method. For help, contact us at masale@gmail.com.
