About Agri Masala

Agri Masala is a traditional Indian spice blend originating from the Maharashtra region. This authentic blend combines the perfect balance of heat, aroma, and flavor to enhance any dish. Our recipe has been perfected over generations to deliver the rich, complex flavors that make Indian cuisine so beloved worldwide.

Each batch is carefully crafted using traditional methods to preserve the authentic taste and aroma of the spices. Our masala contains no artificial colors, flavors, or preservatives - just pure, natural spices.

Usage & Pairings

Perfect for curries, marinades, grilled meats, and vegetable dishes. Use 1-2 teaspoons per serving to enhance flavor without overwhelming. Best paired with:

  • Chicken, lamb, and goat curries
  • Vegetable stir-fries and sautéed dishes
  • Rice and lentil preparations
  • Marinades for tandoori and grilled items
  • Traditional Indian snacks and street foods
Health Benefits

Agri Masala not only enhances flavor but also offers health benefits through its natural ingredients:

  • Turmeric contains curcumin with anti-inflammatory properties
  • Cumin aids digestion and is rich in iron
  • Coriander may help lower blood sugar and cholesterol
  • Black Pepper enhances nutrient absorption
  • Red Chilies contain capsaicin that may boost metabolism
  • Garam Masala blend contains antioxidants from various spices
Storage & Shelf Life

For maximum freshness and flavor retention:

  • Store in an airtight container in a cool, dark place
  • Keep away from direct sunlight and moisture
  • Best used within 6 months of opening
  • Manufactured date and expiration clearly printed on packaging
  • Do not store near strong-smelling substances as spices absorb odors

Note: Natural spices may clump together over time. This is normal and does not affect quality. Simply break apart before use.
