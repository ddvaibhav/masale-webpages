Authentic Misal Pav

With our signature Goda Masale blend

★★★★★ 4.8 (124 reviews) ⏱ 45 min

About This Recipe

Misal Pav is Maharashtra's beloved spicy sprouted bean curry served with pav (bread rolls). Our authentic preparation uses the traditional Goda Masale blend that gives it the perfect balance of heat, sweetness, and earthiness.

🍛 Serves 4 🔥 Medium Spice 🕒 Prep 20 min, Cook 25 min

Ingredients

For the authentic misal experience

For Goda Masala:

(Use our special blend or make your own)

2 tbsp our Goda Masale
(or homemade blend)
For Misal:
1 cup sprouted matki (moth beans)
1 large onion, finely chopped
2 tomatoes, pureed
4-5 garlic cloves, minced
1-inch ginger, grated
2 tbsp oil
1 tsp mustard seeds
1 tsp cumin seeds
8-10 curry leaves
1 tsp turmeric powder
1 tbsp jaggery
Salt to taste
For Serving:
Pav (bread rolls)
Farsan (crispy topping)
Chopped onions
Lemon wedges
Fresh coriander

Instructions

Step-by-step guide to authentic Misal Pav

1
Prepare the Sprouts

Pressure cook sprouted matki with 1/2 tsp turmeric and salt (3 whistles). Drain and set aside.

2
Tempering (Tadka)

Heat oil, add mustard seeds, cumin, and curry leaves. When they splutter, add onions and sauté. Then add ginger-garlic paste.

3
Add Spices & Cook

Add tomato puree and cook until oil separates. Then add Goda Masale, turmeric, jaggery. Cook 2–3 mins.

4
Combine & Simmer

Add sprouts and 2 cups water. Boil, then simmer for 10–15 mins. Adjust salt & spice.

5
Prepare Accompaniments

Chop onions, coriander, lemon wedges. Toast pav in butter if desired.

6
Serve Hot

Layer farsan in a bowl, pour hot misal, top with farsan, onion, coriander, lemon. Serve with pav.

Chef's Tips:
  • • Add 1/4 cup grated coconut with tomato for richness
  • • Goda Masala includes dagad phool (black stone flower)
  • • Let misal sit for 30 mins after cooking for deeper flavor
