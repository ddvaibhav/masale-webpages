Shipping & Delivery

Delivery Areas

We deliver to all major cities and towns across India. Our standard delivery covers:

  • All metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad)
  • Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities nationwide
  • Most Tier 3 cities and towns
  • Rural areas with access to major transportation routes

For remote locations, delivery times may be longer. Contact us to confirm delivery availability in your area.

Shipping Charges

Our shipping charges are based on order value and delivery location:

  • Free Shipping on orders above ₹999
  • Flat ₹49 for orders between ₹499-₹999
  • Flat ₹99 for orders below ₹499
  • Express Delivery - Additional ₹149 regardless of order value

Shipping charges for bulk orders will be calculated based on weight and destination.

Return & Exchange Policy

Return Eligibility

We accept returns under the following conditions:

  • Damaged, defective, or incorrect product received
  • Seal broken or tampered packaging
  • Product quality doesn't match description
  • Expired or near-expiry products

Note: We do not accept returns for change of mind or if the product has been opened and used.

Return Timeframe

You can initiate returns within:

  • 7 days of product delivery for quality issues
  • 3 days for incorrect product received
  • 1 day for expired products

To initiate a return, please contact our customer support with your order details.

Return Process

Step 1: Contact Customer Support

Email us at returns@agrimasala.com or call +91 98765 43210 within the eligible timeframe.

Step 2: Return Authorization

We'll provide a Return Authorization Number and instructions for returning the product.

Step 3: Package & Ship

Securely pack the product in its original packaging and ship it to our facility.

Step 4: Inspection & Resolution

We'll inspect the returned item and process your refund or replacement within 5 business days.

Refund Policy

Refund Methods

Refunds will be issued through the original payment method:

  • Credit/Debit Card payments: Refund to source card (5-7 business days)
  • UPI payments: Refund to source UPI ID (24-48 hours)
  • Net Banking: Refund to source account (3-5 business days)
  • Cash on Delivery: Refund via bank transfer or UPI (provide details)

Refund Processing Time

Once we receive and inspect your return:

Refund Type Processing Time Additional Notes
Quality Issues 3-5 business days After inspection
Incorrect Product 1-3 business days After receiving return
Expired Product 24 hours Photo evidence required

Exchange Policy

We offer exchanges for:

  • Damaged or defective products
  • Incorrect items shipped
  • Wrong variant or size (if applicable)

Exchanges are subject to product availability. If the replacement product is unavailable, we'll issue a full refund.

Shipping charges for exchanges will be borne by us for our errors, but for size/color exchanges, customers bear return shipping costs.
