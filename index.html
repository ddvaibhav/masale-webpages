Spiced Chicken Curry

Indulge in the rich and aromatic flavors of our Spiced Chicken Curry. This recipe combines tender chicken pieces with a blend of traditional Indian spices simmered to perfection in a creamy tomato-based sauce.

Fun Facts

  • The turmeric in this curry contains curcumin, which is a powerful anti-inflammatory agent.
  • Garam masala is a warm spice blend that varies regionally and elevates the dish's complexity.
  • Coconut milk adds creaminess and mellows the heat of the spices beautifully.

Paneer Masala

Paneer Masala is a classic North Indian dish that brings together the richness of paneer (Indian cottage cheese) with the deep flavors of a spiced tomato-onion gravy. This vegetarian favorite features soft, succulent paneer cubes simmered in a creamy base infused with aromatic spices like cumin, garam masala, turmeric, and dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi).

Fun Facts

  • Paneer is a rich source of protein and calcium, especially valuable in vegetarian diets.
  • Unlike most cheeses, paneer doesn’t melt, making it perfect for hearty curries and grilling.
  • Paneer Masala is sometimes called Shahi Paneer when prepared with added cream, nuts, and a more indulgent spice base.
